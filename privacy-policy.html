Privacy Policy

Fuel Finder — Last updated 3 October 2026

Fuel Finder is a free app that shows nearby fuel prices. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

What we collect

No account is required. We do not create user profiles or keep location histories. Shared fuel-price caches contain station information without user or device identifiers.

Location

The app requests your location while in use solely to find fuel stations near you. Your coordinates are sent as a query to our server to calculate distances and return nearby results. They are:

  • Not saved in the application's database or application request logs
  • Not sent to fuel-price providers as precise user coordinates
  • Discarded immediately after the response is sent

For German and Dutch prices, we round the search centre to a shared area on an approximately one-kilometre grid and send that centre to Tankerkönig or Petromap respectively. We cache the area's station data so nearby searches can reuse it. The area cache does not contain a user identifier, device identifier, or IP address.

You can revoke location access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Fuel Finder. You can still search by town or postcode and open your favourites without location permission. A manually selected place is used as the search centre instead of your device location.

Saved preferences

Your fuel choice and favourites are saved in the app's storage on your device. UK and German favourites include station names, addresses and coordinates. Dutch favourites retain only a station identifier; their details are requested from our server when displayed. Favourites do not save fuel prices or your location. You can remove favourites in the app. We do not keep a history of your searches.

Fuel price data

UK prices are sourced from the GOV.UK Fuel Finder API. German prices come from Tankerkönig, and Dutch prices from Petromap. We cache station and price data on our server to reduce repeated provider requests. Dutch results expire after three hours and expired data is removed regularly. No user identifier is associated with these caches.

Third-party services

Town and postcode queries are sent to Apple Maps to find matching places. The selected place's coordinates are sent to our server to find fuel prices. Maps displayed in the app and driving directions opened in Apple Maps use Apple's services and are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Hosting providers may generate infrastructure and access logs under their own policies. The application disables request logs that would contain precise coordinates or provider API keys.

No analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking frameworks are included in the app.

Children

Fuel Finder does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
